# Contributing to Sanggar Digital

This is currently a hackathon concept repository. Contributions are welcome, especially from dancers, sanggars, cultural educators, accessibility practitioners, and engineers.

## Before contributing

- Explain the user or cultural problem your change addresses.
- Preserve attribution and permission for contributed materials.
- Do not add videos, music, datasets, or logos unless their usage rights are clear.
- Prefer small, testable changes over speculative features.

## Product principles

- Cultural context stays attached to movement references.
- Pose feedback is guidance, not an absolute definition of correctness.
- Safety and moderation are requirements for social features.
- A working, narrow demo beats a broad unfinished platform.

## Pull requests

Include:

- What changed
- Why it matters
- How it was tested or reviewed
- Any rights, consent, attribution, or cultural considerations
